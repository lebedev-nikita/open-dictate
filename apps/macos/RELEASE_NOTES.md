OpenDictate for macOS **0.5.0-rc.26** removes the global reset button from post-processing settings. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Post-processing settings now end with the value-formatting controls, matching the removal of Reset settings on Android.
- Punctuation and value-formatting switches, custom rules and the dedicated dictation shortcut retain their existing behavior.

Download the universal DMG and replace OpenDictate in Applications. This release candidate is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. Verify the attached SHA-256 checksum.
