OpenDictate for macOS **0.8.2** sorts the dictionary. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Dictionary terms are sorted alphabetically, ignoring case, when the Dictionary page opens or closes and when a selection is added. **Clean up** sorts as well. Sorting never happens while you type.

Upgrade with Homebrew or download the universal DMG and replace OpenDictate in Applications. This release is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. Verify the attached SHA-256 checksum.
