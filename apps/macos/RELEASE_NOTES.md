OpenDictate for macOS **0.9.2** makes disabled buttons readable. Requires macOS 14.2+ on Apple Silicon or Intel.

- Disabled buttons such as **Save**, **Start Recording** and **Restore default shortcuts**, and the shortcut recorder during dictation, now have clearly legible titles in both light and dark themes while still looking inactive.

Upgrade with Homebrew or download the universal DMG and replace OpenDictate in Applications. This release is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. Verify the attached SHA-256 checksum.
