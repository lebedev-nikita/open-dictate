OpenDictate for macOS **0.8.1-rc.1** edits word replacements in place. Requires macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon or Intel.

- The pencil button opens a rule for editing in its own row instead of in the form at the top, so the page no longer moves. Return saves and Escape cancels.
- The form for a new replacement keeps its draft while a rule is being edited.

Download the universal DMG and replace OpenDictate in Applications. This release candidate is ad-hoc signed and **not notarized**. Verify the attached SHA-256 checksum. The stable Homebrew release remains unchanged.
